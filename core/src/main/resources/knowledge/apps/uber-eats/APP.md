## What it is
Uber Eats is a food delivery app, package `com.ubercab.eats`.

## Screens
There are Home/search, restaurant menus, Cart and Orders. Restaurant delivery and Grocery, Convenience, Retail and so on are separate channels.

## Features
- **Restaurants**: shown for the delivery address, with name, rating, reviews, estimated delivery time, delivery fee, offers, open status and links; there are Delivery and Pickup modes. Closed restaurants have no estimated delivery time.
- **Menus**: dish names, categories, prices in the local currency, options and sold-out state. Usually visible without signing in.
- **Cart**: shows options, quantities, minimum order, each fee, address and unit details, and the total. Each tap on Add adds one.
- **Orders**: Orders has past orders and delivery progress of current ones.

## Known behaviour
- Restaurants and grocery or retail items are in different channels.
- Sign-in or verification may appear.
