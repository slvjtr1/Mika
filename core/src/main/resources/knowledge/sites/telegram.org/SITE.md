## What it is
A messaging service with private chats, groups and channels. The web version is at `web.telegram.org`; the matching app knowledge is `telegram`. You need to be signed in to use it.

## Pages and URLs
Web version `https://web.telegram.org/k/`: search and the conversation list on the left, the chat on the right. Sign-in is by QR code, or phone number plus code; with two-step verification on, a password is also needed.

## Features
- **Read**: the conversation list shows the last message and time; in a conversation, scroll up for earlier messages, with sender, group members and dates. The preview in the list is not the full text.
- **Send**: type directly in an existing conversation; for a new one, find the recipient through search, contacts or new message.

## Known behaviour
- Groups, channels and contacts can share a name.
- Opening a conversation may change its unread state.
